The brave boy climbed the tall hill. At the top he could see the whole town below. He felt happy and proud.
A girl found a secret path in the forest. She followed it past the tall trees. At the end was a beautiful garden.
The children went on a boat ride down the river. They saw fish jumping out of the water. It was the best day ever.
A boy and his dog walked to the beach. They built a sand castle together. The waves came and washed it away.
The explorer opened an old wooden door. Behind it was a room full of golden coins. She could not believe her eyes.
Two friends climbed to the top of the mountain. They could see clouds below them. The world looked small from up there.
A girl rode her bicycle to the lake. She sat under a tree and read her book. The birds sang songs around her.
The boy sailed a small boat across the pond. The wind pushed the sail forward. He pretended to be a sea captain.
A group of children followed a rainbow. They walked through fields and over hills. At the end they found wild flowers.
The girl discovered a cave behind the waterfall. She went inside with her flashlight. The walls sparkled like diamonds.
