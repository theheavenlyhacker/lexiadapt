The morning sun rose over the green mountains and painted the sky with orange and pink. All the animals in the forest began to wake up from their long sleep.
A gentle rain fell on the garden and made the flowers grow taller. The butterflies danced between the colorful petals while the bees collected sweet nectar.
The river flowed through the valley carrying leaves and small branches. Fish jumped in the cool water trying to catch insects that flew above the surface.
In the deep forest a family of deer walked quietly between the ancient trees. The baby deer followed its mother learning where to find the best grass to eat.
The ocean waves crashed against the rocky shore sending white foam into the air. Seagulls flew in circles looking for fish near the shallow water by the beach.
After the storm a beautiful rainbow stretched across the entire sky from one mountain to the other. The children ran outside to see the amazing colors shining in the sunlight.
The old oak tree in the park had been growing for over a hundred years. Its branches were so wide that twenty children could play in its cool shade at the same time.
A small stream began high up in the snowy mountains and traveled all the way down to the warm valley below. Along the way it passed through forests and meadows full of wildflowers.
The autumn wind blew through the trees shaking loose the red and golden leaves. They floated down slowly like tiny dancers performing on an invisible stage.
On a clear night the stars filled the dark sky like millions of tiny candles. The full moon shone so brightly that you could see your shadow on the ground below.
